This User Training Manual for the KRIB Rental Management System is designed to help landlords and tenants understand how to navigate and use the platform effectively. It describes the system's functionalities, provides step-by-step task instructions, and answers common questions. KRIB is a web-based rental management platform designed to digitise and simplify rental operations in Kenya. It enables landlords to manage properties and monitor payments, while tenants can pay rent, submit maintenance requests, and access their rental documents — all from a single, secure online platform.  1. Identifying Your Audience Before using this guide, it is important to understand who it is written for. KRIB serves two primary user types, each with different roles and requirements:  1.1 Target Audience Landlords and Property Managers: Property owners and managers who need to oversee rental units, track payments, manage tenant profiles, and monitor maintenance activities. They typically have basic to intermediate digital literacy and require tools for financial tracking and operational oversight. Tenants: Residents occupying rental properties managed through KRIB. They require a simple, convenient way to pay rent, view their payment history, submit maintenance requests, and access lease documents. Tenants may have varying levels of technical experience, but are assumed to have basic smartphone or computer skills.  1.2 Audience Needs As the KRIB platform serves two distinct audiences, this manual addresses both user types with separate guidance sections. Key questions this manual answers include: How do I register and log in to KRIB? What can I see on my dashboard? How do I pay rent or track payments? How do I submit or respond to a maintenance request? How do I access my lease agreement or payment receipts? What do I do if I encounter an error or problem?   	 2. Getting Started  2.1 System Requirements To use KRIB, you will need the following: An internet-enabled device: a smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer. A stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data). A web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge recommended). A registered KRIB account (see Section 2.2 for registration instructions).  2.2 Registering on KRIB If you are a new user, follow these steps to create your account: Open your web browser and navigate to the KRIB platform URL. On the home screen, click the 'Register' button. Fill in the registration form with your full name, email address, phone number, and a secure password. Select your user role: choose 'Landlord' if you are a property owner or manager, or 'Tenant' if you are renting a property. Click 'Submit' to create your account. You will receive a confirmation notification. You may now log in.  2.3 Logging In Go to the KRIB login page. Enter your registered email address and password. Click 'Sign In'. The system will verify your credentials. If correct, you will be redirected to your rolespecific dashboard. If you have forgotten your password, click 'Forgot Password' and follow the prompts to reset it via your registered email.  3. System Overview KRIB is organised around a role-based structure. The features and information you see depend on whether you are logged in as a Landlord or as a Tenant. Both roles share a common interface structure — a header with the system logo and logout option, a navigation sidebar (menu), and a main content area — but the content displayed differs by role.  The KRIB platform consists of the following core modules: Authentication Module: Manages secure user login, registration, and role-based access. Dashboard Module: Displays a personalised summary of the user's most important information. Rent Payment Module: Enables tenants to pay rent and landlords to track income. Maintenance Request Module: Allows tenants to report issues and landlords to manage responses. Document Management Module: Provides secure storage and retrieval of lease agreements and receipts. Notification Module: Delivers automated alerts for payments, arrears, and maintenance updates.   	 4. Using KRIB — Tenant Guide  4.1 Tenant Dashboard When you log in as a Tenant, you are taken to the Tenant Dashboard. This is your home screen within KRIB. It displays: Your current rent status: whether your rent for the current period is paid, due, or overdue. Your recent payment history: a list of your last payments with dates and amounts. Any open maintenance requests and their current status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved). Any notifications or alerts from your landlord.  4.2 Paying Rent KRIB supports payment via M-Pesa, credit/debit card, and PayPal. To pay your rent: From the navigation sidebar, click 'Pay Rent'. The system displays the amount currently due for the billing period. Select your preferred payment method: M-Pesa, Card, or PayPal. Enter the required payment details (e.g., your M-Pesa phone number or card details). Confirm the payment amount and click 'Pay Now'. The system sends your payment request to the selected payment gateway for processing. If the payment is successful, you will receive a digital receipt on screen and an SMS confirmation on your registered phone number. Your dashboard balance is updated immediately to reflect the payment.  Note: The payment amount must exactly match the rent due for the current billing period. The system will reject any incorrect amounts. If your payment fails, check your M-Pesa balance or card details and try again.  4.3 Viewing Payment History From the navigation sidebar, click 'Payment History'. A list of all your past payments is displayed, showing the payment date, amount, method, and transaction code. You can download or view a digital receipt for any past payment by clicking the receipt icon next to the relevant entry.  4.4 Submitting a Maintenance Request If there is an issue with your rental property that requires attention, you can report it through KRIB: From the navigation sidebar, click 'Maintenance Requests'. Click the 'Add New Request' button. Enter a clear description of the issue (e.g., 'Leaking roof in bedroom', 'Broken water pipe'). Select the urgency level: Low, Medium, or High. Optionally, attach a photo of the issue using the upload button. Click 'Submit Request'. Your request is logged in the system with a 'Pending' status, and your landlord is notified automatically. You can track the status of your request from the Maintenance Requests section of your dashboard.  4.5 Accessing Documents From the navigation sidebar, click 'Documents'. A list of your available documents is displayed (e.g., your current lease agreement, past receipts). Click on any document to view or download it. Note: You can only access documents associated with your property. Attempting to access documents of another tenant's property is not permitted and will be blocked by the system.   	 5. Using KRIB — Landlord Guide  5.1 Landlord Dashboard When you log in as a Landlord, you are taken to the Landlord Dashboard. This displays: A summary of your properties: number of occupied and vacant units. Total rent collected for the current period and any outstanding arrears. A list of tenants with overdue payments (if any), flagged automatically by the system. Pending maintenance requests from tenants across all your properties. Recent notifications and alerts.  5.2 Adding a Property From the navigation sidebar, click 'Properties'. Click 'Add New Property'. Enter the property name, location, and rent amount. Click 'Save Property'. The property is now registered in the system. You can then assign a tenant to the property (see Section 5.3).  5.3 Adding a Tenant Before a tenant can use KRIB for your property, they must be associated with your property through a lease: From the navigation sidebar, click 'Tenants'. Click 'Add Tenant' and enter the tenant's details (name, email, phone). Select the property and unit to assign to the tenant. Create a lease agreement: enter the start date, end date, and rental terms. Click 'Activate Lease'. The tenant will receive a notification and can now use KRIB to pay rent and submit maintenance requests for that property.  5.4 Monitoring Payments From the navigation sidebar, click 'Payments'. A complete list of all rent payments received is displayed, with tenant name, property, amount, date, and payment method. Any accounts in arrears are highlighted, with the overdue amount clearly indicated. You can generate a payment report for a selected time period by clicking 'Export Report'.  5.5 Managing Maintenance Requests From the navigation sidebar, click 'Maintenance'. A list of all maintenance requests submitted by your tenants is displayed, showing the property, issue description, urgency, and current status. Click on a request to view its full details. Update the status of a request (e.g., change from 'Pending' to 'In Progress' or 'Resolved'). The tenant is automatically notified when the status of their request is updated.   	 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  Q: What payment methods does KRIB support? A: KRIB supports M-Pesa (via Safaricom Daraja API), credit/debit card payments, and PayPal. All payment methods are processed securely through verified payment gateways.  Q: What happens if my rent payment fails? A: If your payment fails, the system will display an error message and the transaction will not be recorded. No payment will be deducted. Check your M-Pesa balance or card details and try again. If the problem persists, contact your landlord or the platform support team.  Q: Can I reset my password? A: Yes. On the login page, click 'Forgot Password'. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your email to create a new password.  Q: Who can see my lease agreement and payment receipts? A: Documents stored on KRIB are accessible only to authorized users — the tenant and the landlord associated with the specific property. The system enforces role-based access control to protect your information.  Q: What does it mean if my account is flagged for arrears? A: If you have not paid your rent by the due date, the KRIB system automatically flags your account as being in arrears. You will see this on your dashboard, and your landlord will also be notified. To clear the arrears, make your rent payment as soon as possible via the 'Pay Rent' feature.  Q: Can I use KRIB on my phone? A: Yes. KRIB is designed with a responsive, mobile-friendly interface. It can be accessed on any smartphone or tablet using a standard web browser without needing to download a separate app.  8.
