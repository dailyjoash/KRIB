Landlords often depend on handwritten records and inperson rent collection, leading to lost information, delayed payments, and tracking inefficiencies. Similarly, tenants experience difficulties in communication, rent tracking, and maintenance requests. In the digital era, there is a growing need for systems that simplify and automate rental management for both parties. 1.2 Problem Statement The Kenyan rental market suffers from inefficiencies caused by manual record-keeping and lack of automation. These challenges include: Missed or delayed rent payments. Lack of proper communication between landlords and tenants. Difficulty in tracking arrears and maintenance requests. These issues result in poor tenant satisfaction and unnecessary administrative burdens for landlords. There is a need for a digital platform that enhances transparency, communication, and efficiency in rental management. 1.3 Proposed solution KRIB aims to simplify the rental process for landlords, tenants, and property managers by providing a centralized, automated, and user-friendly system. Key features of KRIB include: Tenant and Landlord Management: KRIB will maintain detailed profiles for tenants and landlords, including contact information, rental history, and lease agreements. This ensures accurate records and easy access to tenant-landlord information. Property Management: Landlords can list their properties with detailed descriptions, images, and rental terms. KRIB allows for easy property tracking, maintenance scheduling, and lease management, minimizing manual record-keeping. Payment Processing: The platform will enable tenants to make online rental payments and track payment history, while landlords can monitor income and generate invoices. Automated reminders for due payments will reduce late payments and disputes.  Communication and Notifications: KRIB will include in-app messaging and notification systems to streamline communication between tenants, landlords, and property managers. Users will receive alerts about upcoming payments, lease renewals, and maintenance requests. Reporting and Analytics: The system will generate reports on rental income, occupancy rates, and maintenance activities. These insights will support informed decision-making for landlords and property managers. Security and Role-Based Access: KRIB will implement secure authentication and role-based access control, ensuring that tenants, landlords, and property managers access only the relevant sections of the platform. Sensitive data will be protected through encryption and secure login protocols. By implementing KRIB, landlords and property managers can efficiently manage properties, tenants enjoy a transparent and convenient rental experience, and overall rental operations become more streamlined, accurate, and secure. 1.4 Objectives Main Objective To develop and implement KRIB, as a rental management platform that streamlines operations between landlords and tenants by automating rent collection, communication, and maintenance management. Specific Objectives; To automate rent collection and integrate with M-Pesa, card, and PayPal payment options. To provide secure digital storage for lease agreements and rent receipts. To facilitate real-time communication between tenants and landlords. To create a maintenance management module for tracking and resolving issues. To develop role-based dashboards for landlords and tenants. 1.5 Justification The KRIB system addresses key challenges in rental property management by digitizing operations and reducing manual effort. By integrating payment solutions and automating communication, the system improves efficiency, transparency, and satisfaction for both landlords and tenants. This innovation will also support Kenya’s growing digital economy by modernizing real estate management practices. 1.6 Scope and Limitations Scope Deployment for use by landlords and tenants in urban and peri-urban areas. Functionality for rent payment, maintenance requests, and communication. Secure storage and retrieval of rental documents. Accessible on both desktop and mobile devices. Limitations Dependence on stable internet connectivity. Potential learning curve for first-time digital users. Limited access for users without smartphones or internet access.  	 Chapter Two: Literature Review 2.1 Introduction This chapter provides an overview of existing digital property management systems and their relevance to the development of KRIB. It identifies the gaps and limitations in current solutions, guiding the design and implementation of a more localized and efficient platform. 2.2 Review of Related Works Existing Solutions Several property management systems exist globally, such as Buildium and Rentec Direct, which offer automation in rent collection and tenant management. However, these platforms are not tailored to Kenya’s unique payment infrastructure or landlord-tenant dynamics.  Technology Overview KRIB will leverage technologies such as cloud computing, web-based architecture, and mobile-responsive interfaces for accessibility.  Research Gaps Most current systems lack integration with local payment methods like M-Pesa and do not address the communication challenges common in the Kenyan context. 2.3 Theoretical Framework The Agile development model will guide the iterative development of KRIB. This model supports continuous feedback, incremental improvements, and user-centered design throughout the project lifecycle. 2.4 Summary The literature emphasizes the need for digital transformation in property management. KRIB aims to address existing gaps by integrating local payment solutions, modern web technologies, and role-based user experiences tailored for Kenya’s rental market.  	 Chapter Three: Methodology 3.1 Introduction This section outlines the methodology and approach for the successful development and implementation of the KRIB system. 3.2 System Design System Architecture The KRIB platform will follow a client-server architecture with cloud-based data storage to ensure scalability and data security. Modules/Components Rent Payment Module: Integration with M-Pesa, card, and PayPal. Maintenance Management Module: Reporting and tracking maintenance requests. Document Management Module: Secure storage for lease agreements and receipts. Communication Module: Real-time chat, email, and SMS notifications. Dashboard Module: Role-based dashboards for tenants and landlords. 3.3 Tools and Technologies Programming Languages: Python, JavaScript. Frameworks: Django REST Framework for backend, React.js for frontend. Database: PostgreSQL for structured data storage. Authentication: JWT (JSON Web Tokens). Payment Integration: M-Pesa API, PayPal, Card APIs. Hosting: AWS or DigitalOcean cloud platforms. 3.4 Data Collection/Requirements Data Requirements Property details, tenant information, rent payment history, maintenance reports.  Data Sources Manual landlord records digitized and new data entered during system usage.  Data Collection Methods Data will be entered directly by users through web interfaces and validated by the system. 3.5
